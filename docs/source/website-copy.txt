1. Why the current site must change
Verified public content (21 October 2026): The indexed ORVN Labs homepage currently presents PAS as an AI first-touch brokerage tool. It leads with “Control the first-contact layer before delay kills conversion,” lead-leakage diagnostics, PAS qualification/routing/booking, bespoke deployment pricing and early-access positioning for brokerage owners. This is not the LAREI product described in the supplied planning document. Source: https://www.orvnlabs.com/.
Mismatch: Current visitors infer “AI inbound-lead response infrastructure for brokerages.” Future target visitors should understand “AI-assisted professional websites, property portfolios, deal packages, sharing and analytics for real-estate operators.” Both address real estate, but they have different jobs, interface examples, audiences and value proof.
Recommended treatment: Replace the homepage narrative and main navigation with LAREI-led positioning. Inventory the current PAS pages, calculator links, resources and form URLs before changing them. Daniel must decide whether PAS becomes a clearly labelled secondary/legacy page, is archived or is taken down with redirects. Do not claim PAS never existed or delete a useful existing backlink blindly. Remove unsupported current revenue-recovery predictions and any implied performance guarantees from the new product marketing.
Audit limitation: Search-visible live content was checked; the site’s source markup and pixel-accurate desktop/mobile layouts were not fully inspectable. Do not treat this brief as a completed technical/visual crawl.
2. Brand architecture and premium design direction
ORVN Labs: the technology company and trust layer. Primary website identity; avoid describing it as a brokerage, agency or proven large software vendor.
LAREI: the working product name and primary current product direction. Use “LAREI, by ORVN Labs” where the distinction matters.
Audience: established or ambitious wholesalers, licensed agents/brokerages where appropriate, investors, acquisition/disposition operators and their teams. Lead with practical professional value, not “AI for everyone”.
Core differentiator (positioning, not an unproven competitive claim): one structured property record connects a professional’s site, portfolio, property pages, generated content and observable engagement.
Emotional design target: calm competence, control, discretion, trust and quality. Premium does not mean inaccessible or theatrical. The site should feel suitable for a person who sends six-figure property opportunities and cares about accuracy.
Recommended UI language: Editorial SaaS. Near-black #0A0A0F, warm white #F5F1EA, neutral slate #66717A, fine dividers #D8D8D5, and one measured accent such as muted teal #397D77 or warm brass #9A785A after visual testing. Pair one restrained humanist/geometric sans across headers and body (e.g., Inter/Manrope or equivalent), comfortable 16–18px body, large deliberate headlines, strong contrast, no excessive gradient/glass/card effects. Use coherent 8px spacing grid and generous section spacing.
Photo/visual policy: Main hero must show a real app prototype or production screenshot spanning professional public site ↔ property workspace ↔ buyer-facing property page. Mark early prototypes “Product preview / in development.” Use realistic, clearly hypothetical sample properties instead of stock mansion photos. No fake trust badges, fabricated clients, fake adoption figures, false conversion metrics or testimonials. Respect original source material privacy in demos.
Interactive quality: Buttons sized for touch, clear keyboard focus, adequate form labels, mobile navigation without horizontal overflow, lazy-loaded rich media, fast image delivery, reduced-motion preference and WCAG 2.2 AA as implementation goal. The focus should be credible content first, animation second.

6. How it works page — complete copy
Meta title: How LAREI Works | From Business Profile to Published PropertyH1: From fragmented information to a place people can actually review.
Lead: LAREI connects your professional identity, property information, publishing and engagement in one workflow.
Step 1 — Introduce your business. Tell us your role, markets, audience and preferred voice. Choose what belongs on your public professional page.
Step 2 — Review your website draft. LAREI prepares an editable first version. Check names, qualifications, contact details and the projects you want shown before publishing.
Step 3 — Bring in a property. Paste notes, fill in fields or attach supported documents and photographs. AI organizes supported facts into a reviewable record.
Step 4 — Verify and prepare. Correct source conflicts, mark unknowns and choose what is safe to share. Generate a deal sheet and editable marketing description using that approved information.
Step 5 — Publish the right pages. Give each approved property a dedicated URL and choose whether it appears in your public portfolio. Share one property or your broader professional site.
Step 6 — Learn from engagement. See page activity and contact inquiries, then update the presentation while retaining control of the underlying record.
Trust note: A generated description is not a property appraisal, title opinion, license verification or legal disclosure. The professional remains responsible for their representations.

12. FAQ — complete proposed questions and answers
What is ORVN Labs?ORVN Labs is a real-estate technology business developing LAREI, a platform for professional websites, property presentations and the work required to prepare and publish them.
What is LAREI?LAREI is the provisional name of our product for real-estate professionals. It is being designed to connect a branded public profile, selected property portfolio, AI-assisted property records, shareable property pages and engagement insights.
Who is LAREI for?The initial focus is on wholesalers, real-estate agents, investors and acquisitions/dispositions operators who regularly present property or business information to others.
Do I get my own website?That is part of the planned beta experience. Approved users will be able to prepare a professional public page using their business profile and selected portfolio content. The supported URL format will be confirmed during the beta.
Can every deal have its own page?The planned core workflow provides an individual published property page connected to the user’s professional identity. Each page has its own shareable URL and an inquiry route.
Can I use my own domain?Custom-domain support is being evaluated for a later phase and should not be assumed in the first private beta.
Does AI build the entire website for me?LAREI is designed to draft copy and arrange a focused professional page using approved information. Users review the facts, control public sections and publish intentionally. It is not intended to be an unrestricted website builder.
Can I import my ChatGPT information?The intended first approach is to paste relevant context or upload a prepared business summary or writing samples. Automatic access to an external AI account is not part of the confirmed initial scope.
Will the AI learn how I write?Users are intended to be able to save reviewed style preferences and business context for more consistent generated writing. Those preferences should remain visible, editable and private to the relevant workspace.
Does LAREI verify property values or create an ARV?No authoritative appraisal, automated underwriting or independently verified ARV is included in the initial scope. Professionals remain responsible for checking all material investment assumptions.
Does the map show exact parcel boundaries?Not in the initial release. Basic location mapping or a map link is planned. Parcel GIS and surveyed/legal boundary interpretations are separate future work.
Can I hide my address or documents?The planned publication process provides user control over what becomes public. The implemented settings and supported privacy options will be explained in the beta.
Can I market other people’s properties?Users must have the legal authority and necessary permissions to publish property information and imagery, including any agent/brokerage disclosures required in their market. LAREI does not establish that authority for them.
Does LAREI automatically find buyers or send campaigns?No buyer-finding guarantee or mass distribution is part of the first release. It is designed to make approved property pages easy to share and inquiries easy to receive.
Can I see exactly who clicks my link?Analytics can show measurable page activity and tracked clicks. They do not automatically reveal the identity of anonymous visitors. Identifying contact information is collected only when visitors choose to submit it.
Is LAREI a CRM?No. The initial scope emphasizes presentation, publication and basic inquiry visibility rather than a full lead management, transaction or contract system.
Can I use LAREI if I’m not a wholesaler?Yes. The proposed workflow is designed to adapt core presentation features for agents, investors and real-estate operators, with appropriate content choices and disclosures.
Is it available now?LAREI is being developed for a controlled private beta. Public sign-up may not yet be available. Interested professionals can request early access; approval is not guaranteed.
How much does LAREI cost?Pricing has not been announced. Any pricing, plan limits and beta terms will be communicated before a paid commitment is requested.
How can I contact ORVN Labs?Use the contact page or hello@orvnlabs.com if the address remains the confirmed support inbox. We respond to relevant product and early-access questions.

14. Wireframes and build guidance (structural, not pixel designs)
Homepage desktop reading order: nav → hero left copy/right live UI composition → problem → professional site → property page/deal sheet → AI review → voice/context → analytics → 5-step workflow → audience cards → trust → FAQ → CTA → footer. Use no more than one dominant action per viewport; avoid repeating same cards across sections. Mobile stacks copy above the prototype visual and keeps headline, CTA and trust note visible without excessive scrolling.
Professional public page: slim nav → professional hero (name/role/market/contact) → about → service/markets → filterable approved portfolio → contact form → required disclosures/footer. Site can succeed with zero public properties; never show invented inventory.
Property public page: breadcrumb & branding → photo-led hero, status, asking terms → facts grid → summary → location map/fallback → public files & caveats → inquiry form → footer. Mobile sticky inquiry CTA permitted if unobtrusive, accessible and user-selected.
Authenticated dashboard: sidebar or compact mobile nav → Create Property CTA → My Site publish status → properties needing review → recent active properties → inquiries requiring response → light analytics. Secondary charts must not crowd the work queue.
Designer deliverables: Figma equivalent responsive frames for marketing homepage, product, how-it-works, use cases, early access, sign in, professional page, property page, workspace dashboard, intake/review, empty/loading/error and share modal; mobile versions for the 5 critical screens. Component library with tokens, typography, buttons, inputs, tables, galleries, status chips and dialogs.
15. Migration, SEO and launch checks
Content migration: inventory old PAS landing routes, blog/resource links, lead leakage scorecard, full calculator, FAQ and signup flows. Decide per route: keep as secondary / archive with explanatory page / permanent redirect to nearest relevant page. Never redirect a research resource to an unrelated product just to simplify navigation. Keep access to historic legitimate records where needed.
Content governance: Replace “flagship PAS” language on LAREI-led pages. Do not market live PAS behavior as LAREI features or silently transfer PAS pricing and claims. No fabricated customer results, brand partner logos, investor returns, pricing or product screenshots. Add “in development” language wherever beta functionality is shown before release.
Search and previews: unique page titles, descriptions, favicon, OG image, social preview, sitemap, robots rules. Do not index private drafts, invitation links, AI context or private files. For public professional/property pages, indexability should be a deliberate beta decision (default privacy-conscious). Use canonical URLs after slug or domain migration. Ensure old orvnlabs.com URLs use an explicit redirect plan.
Acceptance: a first-time visitor can identify target users and product in 5–10 seconds; no CTA to a dead signup flow; early-access data lands in review queue; mobile site reads professionally; live screenshots match delivered features; sign-in and invitation works; public site/property navigation follows approved routes; accessibility audit performed against WCAG 2.2 AA; no private details in SEO metadata or image previews; all published FAQ statements reflect current implementation.
16. Measurement plan for redesign (proposed, not achieved results)
Track useful marketing events: CTA click (hero and footer distinguished), Product page view, How-it-works depth, Early access form start, successful application, invitation approval, successful onboarding. Later correlate source campaigns with properly consented applications rather than claim page views equal qualified clients. During beta, assess application quality based on role and active workflow, not vanity signup volume. Gather qualitative feedback from prospective customers on whether the site looks credible enough to send to business partners.
